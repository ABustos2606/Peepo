The xruns are not MIDI's: "client = ingen was not finished" about every 30 s,
the same as before the install (44 to 57 xrun lines an hour in the two hours
before it, with no MIDI service running), with the Pi undervolted and
throttled (vcgencmd get_throttled = 0x50005). "No xruns with a MIDI device on
the bus" cannot be judged until the power supply is replaced.

The virtual card is a stand-in with two limits, so the check works around
them: it cannot be unloaded while a2jmidid holds it (the check stops
peepo-a2j around the unload), and it does not hand sequencer events to a raw
reader (amidi saw no clock; the clock is checked through ALSA's Midi Through
instead: peepo-midi:clock_out -> a2j Midi Through (playback) -> ALSA -> a2j
Midi Through (capture), 53 clock messages in 1 s at 128 BPM). A note written
into the card arrived in JACK on its a2j port, wired to ingen:midi_in.

Found on the Pi and fixed: after install the two new units failed with
"Dependency failed": bound to JACK, they were stopped when JACK restarted and
nothing started them again. peepo-jack.service now Wants peepo-a2j and
peepo-midi, as it already Wants the engine. A deliberate
"systemctl restart peepo-jack" then brought back every unit, the MIDI ports and
the wiring by themselves; the UI restored the startup patch (Clouds guitar, no
tempo of its own: 120 BPM) and peepo-midi followed it.

Seen, not changed: that JACK restart's first attempt failed with "The playback
device hw:CODEC is already in use"; PipeWire and WirePlumber run in the patch
user's session (started by SSH logins) and took the interface in the gap. JACK
retried two seconds later and got it.
