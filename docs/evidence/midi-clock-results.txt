Intervals are exact: 1000 frames at 120 BPM, 4000 at 30, 400 at 300, 1200 at
100; 1379 or 1380 at 87 (1379.31). Each change takes effect from the next
clock message: old intervals, then only new ones, nothing in between.

Found and fixed during the run: loading a patch first set the default tempo
(120), then the bundle set its own, so peepo-midi briefly took 120 between the
old patch and the new one (seen in its log on the first run). LoadPatch now
carries the bundle's tempo and the adapter sets that one before the engine
reads the bundle; the second run's log goes 100 -> 87 directly.

The probe, Python under WSL, itself misses periods ("JackEngine::XRun: client
= clockprobe was not finished" in jackd's log, 18 on an earlier run, none for
peepo-midi or ingen). An earlier run without the exclusion showed an interval
of 0 and one of exactly two intervals at those moments. The probe now records
its own xruns and leaves out intervals within 0.1 s of one (203 of about 1300
here). Measuring on the Pi, against devices, is still to do.
